# Viral Archetypes Catalog / 六大爆款短视频模板库

Use these six proven short-video archetypes when the user wants a high-retention structure modeled after trending Roblox-style shorts on Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and TikTok. Each template is pre-configured in `scripts/shot_compiler.mjs`.

## 1. `daily-meltdown` · 打工人/早八学生破防日常

- **Why it works**: Combines cute glossy blind-box visuals (`chibi-plush`) with relatable everyday exhaustion (Monday alarm, deadline crash, empty wallet, late-night homework).
- **Recommended Rig & Shader**: `chibi-blocky` + `glossy-vinyl`
- **4-Shot 15s Beat Structure**:
  1. **0.0s–3.5s (Hook)**: Extreme close-up on a vibrating miniature red alarm clock; a yellow/cream blocky head pops into frame with hollow shocked 2D eyes.
  2. **3.5s–7.5s (Escalation 1)**: The chibi blocky avatar swings a rigid C-shaped hand down to smash the alarm clock, sending plastic number blocks flying.
  3. **7.5s–11.5s (Escalation 2)**: Seated at a miniature matcha-toned desk, the avatar stares at a glowing laptop screen as waterfall tear decals appear on its cheeks.
  4. **11.5s–15.0s (Payoff / Loop)**: The avatar pivots 90 degrees forward at the waist hinge and face-plants flat onto the keyboard with a comical bounce.

## 2. `duo-comedy` · 双人反差闺蜜/冤家小剧场

- **Why it works**: Visual contrast between two distinct avatars in the same frame: one hyper-styled UGC streetwear/princess avatar and one minimalist yellow Classic Noob avatar.
- **Recommended Rig & Shader**: `r15` or `chibi-blocky` + `matte-abs`
- **4-Shot 15s Beat Structure**:
  1. **0.0s–3.5s (Hook)**: Split medium shot: the fancy UGC avatar strikes a glamorous pose while the Classic Noob stands completely stiff with a `-_-` face decal.
  2. **3.5s–7.5s (Escalation 1)**: The fancy avatar attempts a cool spin while holding a miniature boba cup, accidentally bumping the Noob with its boxy backpack.
  3. **7.5s–11.5s (Escalation 2)**: The Noob tips over like a solid wooden plank at a 45-degree angle while keeping the exact same deadpan 2D smile.
  4. **11.5s–15.0s (Payoff)**: Both avatars freeze and slowly rotate their cylindrical heads toward the camera in synchronized awkward silence.

## 3. `obby-storytime` · 沉浸式跑酷配吐槽故事会

- **Why it works**: High-retention visual pacifier for voiceover storytelling, gossip, or study tips. Pastel sky obstacle blocks + crisp jumping physics keep viewers watching to the end.
- **Recommended Rig & Shader**: `r6` + `matte-abs`
- **4-Shot 15s Beat Structure**:
  1. **0.0s–3.5s (Hook)**: Dynamic third-person follow shot behind an R6 avatar leaping across glowing pastel-pink sky blocks above a fluffy cloud sea.
  2. **3.5s–7.5s (Escalation 1)**: Low-angle tracking shot as the avatar hops rapidly across three spinning mint-green cylinder platforms with stiff swinging arms.
  3. **7.5s–11.5s (Escalation 2)**: The avatar pauses at the edge of a disappearing glass bridge, looking down as its 2D face decal switches to panic.
  4. **11.5s–15.0s (Payoff / Loop)**: The avatar takes a giant rigid leap onto the next spawn pad, landing in the exact starting pose of Shot 1 for a seamless loop.

## 4. `mini-tycoon` · 治愈系微缩咖啡馆/小店经营

- **Why it works**: Satisfying tactile ASMR aesthetic. Isometric miniature rooms, snapping blocky furniture, and warm cozy lighting perform strongly on Xiaohongshu.
- **Recommended Rig & Shader**: `chibi-blocky` + `clay-soft`
- **4-Shot 15s Beat Structure**:
  1. **0.0s–3.5s (Hook)**: 45-degree isometric macro view of a miniature sun-lit bakery counter; a chibi blocky chef pops up behind the cash register.
  2. **3.5s–7.5s (Escalation 1)**: Close-up as fingerless C-shaped hands place a glossy blocky croissant and a miniature latte cup onto a pastel tray with a crisp snap.
  3. **7.5s–11.5s (Escalation 2)**: A tiny blocky customer with a cat-ear beanie waddles up to the counter, starry-eyed decal gleaming under warm pendant lamps.
  4. **11.5s–15.0s (Payoff)**: Pull-back diorama shot as golden coin blocks float upward above the cozy café roof.

## 5. `outfit-drop` · 穿搭卡点变装秀 (Dress-to-Impress Style)

- **Why it works**: Built for music beat drops. Starts with a plain gray blocky base avatar and cuts on a spin/jump into a high-fashion UGC look under runway spotlights.
- **Recommended Rig & Shader**: `r15` + `glossy-vinyl`
- **4-Shot 15s Beat Structure**:
  1. **0.0s–3.5s (Hook)**: A plain gray blocky avatar stands on a dim runway podium looking shy with a sweat-drop 2D decal as a spotlight flickers on.
  2. **3.5s–7.5s (Escalation 1)**: Camera orbits rapidly as the avatar leaps upward into a glowing ring of floating blocky clothes and star particles.
  3. **7.5s–11.5s (Escalation 2)**: Match-cut landing on the beat drop: the avatar now wears a glossy Y2K cyber puffer, oversized headphones, and star glasses with a confident smirk decal.
  4. **11.5s–15.0s (Payoff)**: Low-angle hero shot as camera flashes pop around the runway and the avatar holds a victory peace pose with C-shaped hands.

## 6. `meme-slapstick` · 抽象定格梗图小剧场

- **Why it works**: Fast, absurd physical comedy built on the contrast between stiff R6 geometry and dramatic cinematic lighting shifts.
- **Recommended Rig & Shader**: `r6` + `glossy-vinyl`
- **4-Shot 15s Beat Structure**:
  1. **0.0s–3.5s (Hook)**: Bright cheerful room; a Classic Noob blocky avatar proudly holds up a burnt black blocky pizza with a cheerful default smile decal.
  2. **3.5s–7.5s (Escalation 1)**: Instant lighting cut to dramatic film-noir top spotlight; the camera crash-zooms into the unchanging painted smile.
  3. **7.5s–11.5s (Escalation 2)**: A tiny wisp of stylized blocky smoke rises from the pizza while the avatar's left arm block detaches and clatters onto the table.
  4. **11.5s–15.0s (Payoff)**: Locked-off wide shot as the entire avatar tips sideways at 90 degrees onto the floor without bending a single joint.
